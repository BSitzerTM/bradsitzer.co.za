PowerWalk

You lead a small group of neighbours with one demand. Public opinion starts in the middle of the map. Walk the board, listen to people, and pitch to the right ones in the right way. Push opinion to the green line before round 3 ends.

Move WASD or arrows. Jump Space (grab the gold sparks floating overhead, they double your next pitch). Cards 1 / 2 / 3. Act E: with no card, listen; with a card, pitch.

Every character needs something different, and each need matches a principle from Nonviolent Communication or Sociocracy: 👀 Observe and ask (NVC: facts without blame, then a clear request), 💛 Name needs (NVC: feelings and needs, with empathy), 🤝 Seek consent (Sociocracy: small pilot, invite objections, set a review date). Listening is free. Guessing wrong wastes a pitch. Two matches in a row earn a bonus spark.
